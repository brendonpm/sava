## English (default)

**Name:** Sava — pixel art companion for AI chats
**Summary:** Sava, a pixel art character who reacts to Claude and Gemini replies with 18 expressions. Free and private.

```
Sava is a pixel art character who sits in the corner of your screen while you chat with Claude or Gemini — and reacts to every AI reply.

She laughs at jokes, blushes at compliments, panics when you say you dropped the database without a backup, and melts when a kitten shows up.

WHAT SHE DOES
• 18 expressions: happy, embarrassed, angry, sad, excited, surprised, confused, thoughtful, in love, scared, sleepy, wink and more.
• Motion and effects for each emotion: hearts, stars, music notes, puffs of angry smoke, rain, "zzz"…
• Intensity from 1 to 3: a strong emotion gets more motion and more particles.
• Talks (moves her mouth) while the AI writes.
• Pays attention to you: bounces while you type and looks thoughtful after you send.
• Naps if you stay idle for a while, and wakes up when you come back.
• 💬 button: one click sends the chat the instruction that makes the AI talk like Sava and tag the emotion of each reply.
• Customizable: size, new emotions, which expression each emotion uses, effects and more.
• Drag her to any corner of the screen; her position is saved.
• Available in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and Japanese.

HOW IT WORKS
The AI gets an instruction to end each reply with a tag like [emo:feliz:2]. The extension reads the tag, changes the expression and hides the tag from the chat. Without the tag, it uses words and emojis in the reply to guess the emotion.

PRIVACY
Everything runs in your browser. The extension has no server, makes no network requests, collects no data and has no ads or tracking. It only reads reply text, on the spot, to pick the expression.

Works on Claude, Gemini and other popular AI chat sites — the full list is in the options.
Independent project, not affiliated with the companies behind these AI chats. All product names are trademarks of their respective owners.
```

## 日本語

```

冗談には笑い、ほめられると照れて、バックアップなしでデータベースを消したと聞けば大あわて。子猫が出てくればメロメロです。

できること
・18種類の表情：うれしい、照れ、怒り、かなしい、わくわく、びっくり、こまった、考え中、恋してる、こわい、ねむい、ウインクなど。
・感情ごとの動きとエフェクト：ハート、星、音符、怒りのけむり、雨、「zzz」…
・強さは1〜3：強い感情ほど、動きもパーティクルも大きくなります。
・AIが書いている間は話します（口が動きます）。
・あなたを見ています：入力中はぴょこぴょこ跳ね、送信後は考え込みます。

Claude、Geminiなど人気のAIチャットで動作します（対応サイトの一覧は設定画面にあります）。
これらのAIチャットの提供企業とは関係のない独立したプロジェクトです。各製品名はそれぞれの所有者の商標です。
```
